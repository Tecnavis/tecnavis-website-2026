The full story

Why Tecnavis exists, and what it's for.

Story

Built by developers who stayed hands-on

We're a working studio, not a sales layer in front of freelancers. The people who scope your project are the same ones writing the code, deploying it, and answering when something breaks. That's not a slogan — it's the only way we've found to keep quality consistent once a project gets past the pitch stage.

Mission

Startup-grade engineering, without the overhead

Growing businesses deserve the same discipline — clean architecture, proper deployment pipelines, real testing — that funded startups get, at a scope and price that fits where you actually are. We'd rather build something right-sized and solid than something impressive-looking and fragile.

Vision

The studio you call for the next project too

We measure success by whether clients come back for the second build, the third feature, the next app — not just the first invoice. A one-time client means we did something wrong somewhere.

Why teams stay with us

Continuity

Same people, start to finish

The developer who builds your backend also ships the app and handles the deployment — nothing gets lost in a hand-off between teams.

Availability

We stay on after launch

Support doesn't end when the invoice is paid. Bugs, small fixes and the next round of features go through the same people who built it.

Fit

Scoped to the size you are now

Practical architecture for where your business is today, built so it doesn't have to be thrown away when you grow into the next stage.
