Account & Data Deletion

How to ask Tecnavis Web Solutions to delete your app account and personal data, and what is deleted or kept.

On this page
1. Who this page is for2. How to request deletion3. What we delete4. What we may keep, and for how long5. Deleting only some data6. Questions

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · Tecnavis Web Solutions Pvt Ltd

Who this page is for

This page is for users of apps and services published by Tecnavis Web Solutions Pvt Ltd who want their account, or some of their data, deleted. If the app offers a delete-account option in its settings or profile screen, you can use that. If not, or if you cannot sign in, use the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to info@tecnavis.in with the subject “Account deletion request”, or use the button below.
  2. Tell us the app name and the mobile number or email address registered to the account.
  3. Say whether you want your whole account deleted or only specific data removed.
  4. We may reply to confirm it is really you, for example by asking you to write from the registered email or confirm an OTP.
  5. We delete the data and confirm by email, normally within 30 days of verifying your request.

Request account deletion

What we delete

  • Your profile: name, mobile number, email address and profile photo.
  • Content you added in the app: addresses, messages, files, photos and documents.
  • Your login credentials and any saved preferences, and your device tokens for push notifications.

What we may keep, and for how long

  • Invoices, payment and transaction records, which we must keep for tax and accounting law, for the period the law requires.
  • Records needed to resolve a dispute, prevent fraud or meet a legal obligation, only for as long as that need lasts.
  • Backups, which are overwritten on a regular cycle. Deleted data is not restored to live systems.

Anything we keep is limited to what is needed and is not used for anything else.

Deleting only some data

If you would like specific data removed without closing your account, tell us what and we will remove it where the app can still work without it.
