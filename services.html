What we build

Five ways in. One team behind all of them.

Every project starts with one of these — but the moment it needs something else, that's already in-house too. No hand-offs to a second agency, no "that's not what we do."

Websites

Marketing sites, dashboards and client portals — built to load fast and hold up as the content grows, not just look good on day one.

  • Custom design, not a reskinned template
  • Built on Laravel, React or Node — whichever fits the content and traffic pattern
  • Content you can actually update yourself after launch
  • SEO fundamentals done properly: structure, speed, metadata

E-commerce

Storefronts with real checkout, inventory and payment handling — not a shopping-cart plugin bolted onto a template.

  • Razorpay / UPI / COD payment flows wired properly
  • Inventory and order management you actually run day to day
  • Mobile-first checkout — most of your traffic will be on a phone
  • Abandoned-cart handling and basic customer accounts

App development

Native-feeling Android and iOS apps in Flutter, taken all the way through Play Store and App Store submission — not handed to you half-finished.

  • One Flutter codebase, both platforms
  • Push notifications, offline handling, real device testing
  • Store listing, screenshots and submission handled for you
  • Post-launch crash monitoring and update cycles

Custom software

Internal tools, ERPs and automations shaped around how your team actually works — not how a generic package assumes it does.

  • Process mapped before a line of code — we sit with how you actually work
  • Role-based access, audit trails, and real permission structures
  • APIs to connect the tools you already use
  • Built to extend, not rebuilt every time a new need shows up

Digital marketing

SEO, content and campaigns aimed at bringing traffic to a product that's actually ready to convert it — usually paired with one of the four above, since a marketing push into a slow or confusing site just wastes the ad spend.

  • Technical SEO baked into the build, not patched on after
  • Content that targets what people in your area are actually searching
  • Campaign setup for Google and Meta ads where it makes sense
Before you ask

Common questions about the work itself.

Do I need to know which service I need before reaching out?

No — most enquiries start as "I need a website" or "I need an app" and turn into something broader once we understand the business. Tell us what you're trying to solve and we'll tell you honestly what that actually requires.

Can you take over a project someone else started?

Yes, regularly. We'll review what exists, tell you plainly whether it's worth continuing or rebuilding, and go from there — no obligation to throw away working code just to start fresh.

Do you only build with Laravel, React, Node and Flutter?

Those are our core, in-house stacks — the ones we can support properly long after launch. If a project genuinely needs something else, we'll say so upfront rather than force-fitting a tool that isn't right.

What happens after the site or app goes live?

We stay on. Bugs, small fixes and the next round of features go through the same people who built it — see the process page for how that works day to day.

Tell us what you're building.

A short brief is enough to start — we'll come back with real questions, not a generic quote.
