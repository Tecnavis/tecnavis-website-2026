Who we are and what this covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Tecnavis Web Solutions Pvt Ltd (“Tecnavis”, “we”, “us”), Malappuram, Kerala 676505, India, collects, uses and protects personal information when you use:

  • our website at www.tecnavis.in;
  • the mobile apps and web apps that we publish under the Tecnavis name (including on Google Play and the Apple App Store); and
  • our enquiry, support and business communications.

Where we build an app or website for a client, that client decides what the product does with your data and their own privacy policy applies to it. This policy covers products that Tecnavis itself publishes and operates.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiries: name, mobile number, email address, the service you need and the project details you type into our contact form.
  • Accounts in our apps: name, mobile number, email address and a password (stored in hashed form, never in plain text), and any profile details you choose to add.
  • Content you submit: messages, addresses, files, photos or documents that you add while using a feature of an app.
  • Support: what you write to us when you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information such as device model, operating system version, app version, language and time zone.
  • Approximate technical data such as IP address, and crash and error logs that help us fix bugs.
  • Basic usage information, for example which screens or features are used.

Device permissions

Our apps ask for a permission (for example location, camera, photos or files, notifications) only when a feature needs it, and only at the moment you use that feature. You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your device settings; the related feature may then stop working, but the rest of the app will keep working.

Which of the items above apply depends on the specific app. The “Data safety” section on Google Play and the “App Privacy” details on the App Store list what each app collects.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and provide quotes and support.
  • To create and manage your account and provide the features of our apps.
  • To process orders, bookings, requests or payments made through an app.
  • To send service messages such as OTPs, confirmations, updates and security alerts, and push notifications you have allowed.
  • To keep our services secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and fix errors.
  • To understand how our products are used so we can improve them.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Legal basis and consent

We process personal data based on your consent (for example when you submit a form or grant a permission), to perform a contract with you, for our legitimate interests in running and securing our services, and to comply with the law. We handle personal data in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and, where it applies to you, the EU/UK GDPR. You can withdraw consent at any time (see “Your rights”).

Who we share information with

We share information only as needed to run our services, and only with:

  • Service providers that work for us, such as hosting and storage, email delivery, form handling (our website contact form is delivered through Web3Forms), push notifications, crash reporting, analytics, maps and payment gateways. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities and advisers where we are required by law, or to protect our rights, users or the public.
  • A successor if Tecnavis is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets; we will tell you if this changes who holds your data.

Our website loads fonts from Google Fonts and scripts from cdnjs, so those providers receive your IP address when a page loads.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our website does not currently use advertising or tracking cookies. Our apps may use local storage on your device to keep you signed in and remember settings. If we add website analytics in future, we will update this section.

How long we keep data

  • Enquiry details: for as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable period after, so that we can follow up on your project.
  • Account data in our apps: while your account is active, and deleted after you ask us to delete it (see Account & Data Deletion).
  • Invoices and transaction records: for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Backups and logs: removed on a regular cycle after the live data is deleted.

Security

We use measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, access controls and regular updates to protect personal data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects you, we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

Your rights and choices

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you, and get a copy.
  • Ask us to correct data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete your data or your account.
  • Withdraw consent you gave earlier, or object to certain processing.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you, as the DPDP Act allows.
  • Complain to us or to the data protection authority in your country.

Email info@tecnavis.in from the address or number registered with us. We may need to verify who you are first, and we aim to respond within 30 days. To delete an account and its data, use our Account & Data Deletion page.

Children

Our services are not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

International transfers

Our service providers may store or process data on servers outside India. Where that happens we rely on providers that apply appropriate safeguards for personal data.

Links to other sites

Our website and apps may link to other websites, such as our client projects and Tecnavis Platform. They have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for how they handle data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change is significant we will tell you through the website or the app.
