How a project moves

Five stops, one continuous build.

No black box in the middle — here's exactly what happens at each stage, and what we need from you to keep it moving.

01

Discover

We map the business, the users and the real constraints before a line of code gets written.

02

Design

Wireframes and interfaces built around how the product will actually get used day to day.

03

Develop

Built on whichever of Laravel, React, Node or Flutter fits the job — backed by proper version control and review.

04

Deploy

Servers, CI, and store submissions — Play Store and App Store — handled end to end, not left for you to figure out.

05

Support

We stay on after launch: monitoring, fixes, and scoping whatever the next iteration needs.

From your side

What each stage actually needs from you.

The technical work is ours. Here's the part that depends on you, so nothing stalls waiting on either side.

01 · Discover

A call or a written brief — whatever's easier for you. We ask about the business itself, not just the feature list, because the two often diverge.

  • Roughly 30–60 minutes of your time, once
  • Access to anything existing — old site, current process, spreadsheets you're replacing

02 · Design

We share wireframes and screens before touching final code, so changes are cheap while they're still just layout.

  • Feedback on 1–2 rounds of screens
  • Brand assets if you have them — logo, colours, photos

03 · Develop

This is the quiet stage — you'll get periodic check-ins rather than daily noise, with a staging link to watch progress whenever you want.

  • A staging link to review as we go
  • Content — copy, product data, prices — as it's ready

04 · Deploy

We handle hosting, domains and store submissions. If you already have a domain or hosting account, we just need access.

  • Domain/DNS access if you already own one
  • A Google Play / Apple Developer account, for app projects
Timelines

How long does this actually take?

How long does a typical website take?

It depends entirely on scope — a focused business site moves faster than a multi-role portal or a store with a large catalogue. We'll give you a real timeline once we've scoped the actual work in the Discover stage, not a generic range that doesn't hold up.

Can you work to a fixed deadline, like an event or launch date?

Yes — tell us the date up front and we'll scope what's realistically achievable by then, rather than overcommitting and missing it.

What if I want changes after the build starts?

Small adjustments are normal and expected. Larger scope changes get discussed openly — what it adds to the timeline, and whether it's worth doing now or after launch.

Do you sign NDAs or contracts before starting?

Yes, on request — just mention it when you reach out and we'll sort the paperwork before any details are shared.

Ready to start the Discover call?

Tell us what you're building — we'll take it from there.
