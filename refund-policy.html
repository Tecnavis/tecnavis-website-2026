Refund & Cancellation Policy

How cancellations and refunds work for Tecnavis Web Solutions projects, plans and apps.

On this page
1. Scope2. Development and marketing projects3. Support, hosting and recurring plans4. Purchases made in our apps5. How to request a cancellation or refund6. Changes

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · Tecnavis Web Solutions Pvt Ltd

Scope

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for services from Tecnavis Web Solutions Pvt Ltd: website, e-commerce, app and software development, digital marketing, and any support or hosting plans. It should be read with our Terms & Conditions.

Development and marketing projects

  • Each project is priced and scheduled in a written quotation or agreement, including the payment milestones. That document sets out what is refundable.
  • If you cancel before we start work, we will refund the amount you paid, less any payment-gateway charges and any third-party costs already incurred for you (such as domains, licences or stock assets).
  • If you cancel after work has started, work completed up to that point is chargeable, and any remaining balance is refunded where applicable.
  • If we cannot deliver what was agreed, we will first try to correct it. If we still cannot, we will discuss a fair refund for the undelivered part.
  • Third-party purchases made for your project (domains, hosting, SSL, paid plugins, ad spend) are paid to those providers and are generally non-refundable by us.

Support, hosting and recurring plans

  • Recurring plans can be cancelled by written notice before the next billing cycle. Fees already paid for the current cycle are not refunded unless the agreement says so.
  • After cancellation we may provide your files or data on request, within a reasonable period.

Purchases made in our apps

  • Subscriptions and in-app purchases made through Google Play or the Apple App Store are billed and refunded by Google or Apple under their policies. Please request those refunds from them directly.
  • For orders or bookings handled by the business that runs an app, refunds follow the policy shown in that app, and are processed by that business.

How to request a cancellation or refund

Email info@tecnavis.in with your name, project or app name, the payment or order reference, and the reason. We will acknowledge within 2 working days. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, and the time it takes to show up depends on your bank or payment provider.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the date at the top shows the latest version.
